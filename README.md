## About

I work with SQL, Python, Power BI and Excel to build data pipelines and reports. My projects focus on e-commerce operations, sales and employee data.

I have an M.S. in Data Science from Pace University and 3+ years in operations at Amazon and Accenture. My work there involved tracking service targets, managing cases and checking data quality.
